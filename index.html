01 / The thesis

The cloud is a detour. Intelligence belongs where the data is born: welded to the machine, deciding in milliseconds.

Deep-tech operations generate more sensor data than any team can read, and the most critical decisions can't wait on a round-trip to a data center. Turbnetic puts the model next to the machine: low latency, full data sovereignty, and resilience even when the network drops.
